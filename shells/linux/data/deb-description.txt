Romlens is a workbench for studying Super NES ROMs. It disassembles the
65816 code, lifts routines to C, draws the graphics and plays the sound
with an emulated SPC700 and DSP, and keeps your names and comments in a
project you can save.
.
An optional tutor answers questions about the ROM you have open. It needs an
API key for a provider you choose, or a model served on your own machine.
.
Romlens never ships or fetches a ROM. Open one you own.
